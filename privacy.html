Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This policy describes the intended information handling of TikTok Auto Poster. If you connect a TikTok account, the application may process authorization tokens and basic account information returned by TikTok, as well as video files, captions, and posting settings you select.

Storage and use

Application settings and authorization data may be stored locally on the computer running the application. Information is used to connect to TikTok at your direction, prepare posts, submit videos and captions you explicitly confirm, and show posting status. Do not share access tokens, refresh tokens, client secrets, or configuration files.

Third-party services

TikTok processes information under its own terms and privacy policy when you use TikTok features. Video sources may have their own terms.

Retention and security

Local history and files may remain on your device until you delete them. Protect your computer and remove local application data if you no longer want it retained.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application operator through the contact method provided with the application.

Before submitting for review: update this policy to reflect the actual released app and provide a real contact method.
